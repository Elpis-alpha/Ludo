Ludo

The race-home board game, played with two spinners. Roll a six to get out, knock rivals back to base, and bring all four chips home first.

Two to four players, against the computer or passing one device around. Your game is saved as you play.

New game

Pick who sits at each corner. You need at least two players.

Safe squares

A chip on a star or a start square can't be captured.

Starting a new game replaces the one you have saved.

      How to play

      • Spin both spinners. Each one lands on a number from 1 to 6.
      • A six lets a chip out. It leaves your base and lands on your start square.
      • Play each number, or both as one. Move one chip by each number, or a single chip by the two added together. Tap a chip, then where it should go.
      • Land on a rival to capture it. Their chip goes back to base.
      • A double spins again.
      • Get all four chips home. A chip needs the exact count, or less, to reach the centre. The game runs until one player is left.

      Game over
